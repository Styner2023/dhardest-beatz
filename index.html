About the producer

Born in Bo City,
raised in Freetown,
now making music
for the world

D’Hardest Beatz was born in Bo City, Sierra Leone, and raised in Murraytown, Freetown. He made his music debut as a member of the group N.O.T.B High School Label, and later opened his own label, Just Entertainment Business. He studied business administration at the Institute of Public Administration University before dropping out to make beats and record vocal references.

He started recording music in 2014 at a studio named Rap Money Records, where he was signed as a record label artist. After a few years, he decided to study sound engineering out of his passion for music.

He came through several difficult stages in his career while helping other talented musicians. Now he has decided to show his own talent to the audience. He dropped his first music video, “Pray For Salone”, under the record label Grind24, where he was head producer — a video carrying a message of peace and love to all Sierra Leoneans.

The following year he released his own music video featuring the young talented artist Bwoy Shazz, with a song titled “Chase the Paper”. He is also working on a new genre he calls “Afrosoco” — instrumentals full of natural African sounds and Bubu melody.

He is a very talented and popular music producer in Sierra Leone.

He now lives and works in the United States, producing for artists in Sierra Leone, across the diaspora and beyond — carrying the Afrosoco sound far past the city it came from.

To know more about D’Hardest Beatz, find him on all social media platforms under his initials — “D’Hardest Beatz”.

Fifteen years behind the desk — from vocal references cut in a Freetown studio to finished records mixed and mastered for release. Every session starts with the artist’s voice, and the beat gets built around it.

Just Entertainment Business exists to find talent and see it all the way through: recording, producing, mixing and developing artists in Sierra Leone and across the diaspora. Afrosoco came out of that work — natural African drums and Bubu melody, made for the world.
